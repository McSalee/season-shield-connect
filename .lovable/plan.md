# GonaAI Climate Takaful Website

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive single-page website for GonaAI that presents its climate protection model to agribusiness partners, grant funders, and Takaful underwriters.

## What I’ll build
- Sticky navigation with the GonaAI / Takaful Climate Tech identity, page links, demo and dashboard actions, plus a prominent partner button.
- An immersive opening section centered on a northern Nigerian farm, satellite weather intelligence, and feature-phone advisories.
- A restrained trust strip for cooperatives, Islamic finance institutions, input suppliers, and outgrower networks.
- An interactive four-tab “How It Works” section with distinct animated visuals for satellite triggers, mutual pooling, voice advisories, and surplus dividends.
- An institutional impact section with the three supplied impact measures and SDG 1, 2, and 13 markers.
- A partner intake form with the supplied fields and audience options.
- A concise FAQ section to support the navigation link and answer likely partner questions without expanding project scope.

## Visual direction
- Editorial climate-finance aesthetic: deep forest, warm harvest gold, clear sky blue, and restrained neutral surfaces.
- Strong, credible typography and data-inspired linework rather than generic technology gradients.
- Responsive layouts, accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.

## Technical details
- Build the experience at the home address using the existing React/TanStack setup.
- Define all colors, typography, shadows, and animations as reusable design tokens.
- Use bundled visual assets and lightweight CSS/React animation; no external backend or data storage.
- Form submission will provide a polished in-page confirmation because no email destination or storage was requested.
- Add unique page metadata for search and social previews.
- Verify the final page at desktop and mobile widths and resolve any preview errors.
